ADE 1.2.90 lets agents wake themselves for PRs and long waits, lets you switch a chat's account, and makes the desktop app smoother.

## Agents and chats

- Agents can watch or ship a PR and wake when it changes, resume after a crash, wait on goals, and offer stop choices; you can restart a session, and MCP calls have timeouts (#1454).
- Switch a running Claude or Codex chat to another signed-in account from the chat actions drawer. The chat keeps its thread (#1456).
- Self-paced wake-up loops show one row per schedule instead of three, and the composer has a status strip (#1458).
- Lanes follow the branch their agent switches to (#1458).

## Performance

- Project switches with a long chat open no longer freeze the app, and a chat scrolled into its history keeps its place (#1457).
- Cheaper looping animations, streaming code blocks, and terminals rendered with WebGL (#1457).
