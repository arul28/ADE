ADE 1.2.117 gives outside agents typed read tools over ADE MCP, lets browser agents upload, drop files, download and paste, makes chat chips useful, and fixes the iPhone Duo in the Apple pane.

## New

- ADE MCP: typed read tools for projects, lanes, files, diffs, git status, pull requests and chats, the project named on every result, shorter scoped help, and the project's own skills (#1868).
- Browser agents can open file pickers behind a button, drop files on dropzones, handle dialogs, download, and paste; every tab records who opened it and when (#1866).
- Chat chips have a menu on hover or right-click, machine chips open a connection card, and `@mcp` mentions work in the prompt box (#1867).
- "What's new" shows the iPhone Duo, the simulator on your iPhone and the Windows desktop (#1864).

## Improved

- The iPhone Duo turns to all four orientations, gets its own dock under the device, and the Tent and Laptop presets show the pose (#1865).
- Chips open on the right machine (#1867).
