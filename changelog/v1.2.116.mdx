ADE 1.2.116 adds a guided setup and tour for new users, lets you drive the Mac's simulator from your phone, and manages simulator runtimes from the Apple panel.

## New

- Onboarding: a first-run setup that connects your agents and brings your projects in, a guided tour of lanes, chats, tools and connections, browsable ADE Tips, and suggestions shown at the right moment (#1857).
- The iOS app drives the Mac's simulator live. View only is the default; press and hold Control to tap, type and use the hardware buttons (#1853).
- The Apple panel lists simulator runtimes with their size and simulators, removes old ones, and folds a real iPhone Duo, including the outer screen (#1852).
- Project notes: every ADE agent gets a short block of what earlier agents learned about running and building the project. Project settings are cleaner (#1855).
- Jev, a small, fast decision model, now decides chat status, model hints, ⌘K results and lane emoji, with the previous behaviour as its fallback (#1858).

## Improved

- Receipt notices in chat (plan mode, queued messages, reconnects) draw as one quiet line instead of a card (#1851).
- In Focus mode, a finished chat no longer holds a busy lane open or counts as waiting for you (#1854).
- Agents are told that their thinking is not their reply, so final summaries land in visible text (#1859).
- The landing page's story film shows the right still while a video loads (#1860, #1861).
