ADE 1.2.105 adds live voice narration, a redesigned model picker, and safe project moves.

- Claude five-hour capacity is balanced across your accounts (#1629).
- Plan mode ends after you approve the plan, and subagents inherit its access (#1622).
- Compact first queues your message instead of timing out (#1621).

## Projects and machines

- Move a project to a new drive safely (#1633).
- Updates never restart a machine that is already current (#1626).

## Music

## Review
