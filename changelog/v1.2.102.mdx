ADE 1.2.102 fixes a chat that could not accept a message during a long turn, and adds phone sign-in and PR video.

## Chat

- You can send a message into a running turn even when the chat's model has not loaded yet. Before, the composer refused to send without a model, and the model picker stays locked during a turn, so the chat was stuck until the turn ended (#1606).
- A chat keeps its model when its pane is reopened, instead of showing "Select model" until the chat summary loads (#1606).

## Accounts

- Sign a machine in from your phone: scan a QR code and approve on the phone, with no password typed on that machine (#1603).

## Pull requests

- A video in a PR plays inside ADE, and the phone shows a PR description's pictures (#1604).
