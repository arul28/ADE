ADE 1.2.137 is the first post-launch fix release: the CTO sees every open PR and the chats on your other machines, machines stay online through a hung drive, and new accounts sign up cleanly again.

## Improved

- The CTO sees every open PR on the repository and the chats running on your other machines, linked work closes by itself, and Learned rows are calmer (#2652).
- Settings › Machines lists ADE Cloud, and cloud sizes say "Uses no credits" (#2651).
- The Machines menu stays quiet about computers that are simply off (#2641).

## Fixed

- New accounts finish signing up again on the web, the desktop app and `ade login` (#2654).
- A machine stays online when one of its drives stops answering, instead of dropping off the account (#2641).
- `ade issue list` with no flags lists your issues instead of failing, and a GitHub sign-in with no repository no longer hides Linear or Jira issues (#2655).
- Smart decisions keep using the ADE bonus after the model service refuses one request (#2655).

## iOS

- Project cards show their logos with a Windows or Linux computer, and chats from a deleted lane no longer show as orphaned sessions (#2642).
- New accounts finish signing up on the iPhone (#2654).
