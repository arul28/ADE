ADE 1.2.98 improves Home and personal Browser flows, makes chats more reliable, and reduces desktop and web client overhead.

## Home and Browser

- Home widgets, contribution activity, personal chats, and Browser flows work more reliably (#1565).

## Chat and sync

- Large screenshots no longer stop a chat turn (#1558).
- A brain does not launch a second process for a chat owned by another brain (#1559).
- Helper chats launched by agents no longer keep Focus lanes busy (#1557).
- Account secrets sync within seconds, with corrected relay budget accounting (#1556).
- Dev apps preserve machines paired to the installed ADE.

## Performance and web

- Reduced idle renders, brain stalls, and Lanes, Files, and chat-switch overhead (#1560).
- Web client starts with a smaller payload; transcript zoom, command redaction, and scene display are improved (#1561).
