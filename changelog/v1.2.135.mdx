ADE 1.2.135 moves ADE Cloud to one credits balance on every plan, gives Automations drafts that survive restarts and notifications that open the run, and lets the CTO react or stay quiet.

## New

- ADE Cloud uses one credits balance on every plan: Free 50, Plus 100, Pro 600 and Max 1,500, with Small computers free and Medium, Large, X-Large and the cloud iPhone drawing credits (#2494, #2488).
- The ADE Cloud iPhone draws only from credits, and the old iPhone-minutes billing is gone (#2505).
- The ADE Cloud page shows what's left, your cloud computers and what happens when credits run out, with Keep going or Pause right in the top card (#2503, #2488).
- Automations keep one unsaved draft per repository through tab switches and restarts (#2504).
- An automation's notification can open the chat, lane or history of the run that sent it, on desktop, the web and the iPhone (#2504).
- The CTO can end a turn with a reaction or no reply, and hands research to background helpers (#2492).
- Sign-in shows the agree line for the Terms and Privacy Policy, and the privacy policy lists all the data ADE collects (#2498).

## Improved

- Automation steps a rule's computer can't run yet are greyed out with the ADE version it needs (#2504).
- ADE Cloud messages, such as a failed payment, show inside the top card with their actions (#2503).
- The ADE Cloud page mentions the iPhone only when your account has it (#2501).
- The pricing and landing pages are shorter and say what Large and X-Large include (#2497).
- Sponsored cards, still for testers only, use Gravity's own design and start on in the US with a notice and Turn off (#2495, #2506, #2490).

## Fixed

- A fresh CTO thread keeps the messages its story hasn't folded yet, and a model switch keeps the conversation (#2493).
- Mac window buttons stay centred in the header at every zoom level (#2491).

## Windows

- An agent can no longer stop or restart the brain it runs in (#2502).

## Linux

- An agent can no longer stop or restart the brain it runs in through systemd (#2502).

## iOS

- The iPhone shows the new credits balance and "When credits run out" on the ADE Cloud page (#2503, #2488).
- The iPhone opens an automation's run from its notification (#2504).
- CTO reactions show on your messages (#2492).
