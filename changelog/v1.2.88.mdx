ADE 1.2.88 keeps running chats on the account they started with, and lets you manage provider accounts from iOS and the web client.

## Accounts

- A running chat stays on its provider account when load balancing would pick another one, and a forked chat stays on its source account (#1444).
- Manage provider accounts from the iOS app and the web client (#1444).
