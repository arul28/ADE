ADE 1.2.92 makes multi-machine connections do what you tap, brings interactive scenes, imports lanes from outside .ade/worktrees, and speeds up Windows again.

## Remote and multi-machine

- Connect, disconnect, switch, and send across machines do what you tapped (#1484).
- Remote viewers see agent-opened PRs and act on the lane's own machine (#1489).
- Update & restart updates the far machine's open ADE app and says whether it landed (#1487).

## Agents and chats

- Interactive scenes stay live in scrollback, agents self-check them, and they can read live ADE data (#1485).
- Imported chats resume their conversation on any provider account (#1482).

## Lanes

- Lanes outside .ade/worktrees: import, CLI project support, and Electron lane deletes (#1481), with edge cases closed (#1486).
- Default-branch auto-pull runs in the brain (#1479).

## Runtime and Windows

- The brain starts when launchd leaves it pending (#1488).
- Windows performance: no brain freezes from process spawns, cheaper spinners and chat switches, and an update that relaunches (#1480, #1483).
